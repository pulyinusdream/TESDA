AIMS NEXUS Identity Hotfix 1

Cause:
AIMS declares SessionManager and AuthenticationService with top-level const in classic scripts.
Those names are global lexical bindings, but they are not properties of window.

The first adapter checked:
window.SessionManager
window.AuthenticationService

That check never became true. After 120 attempts (about 12 seconds), the adapter redirected
back to the NEXUS main page. During that wait, the legacy AIMS login remained visible.

Fix:
- Use direct lexical references: SessionManager and AuthenticationService.
- Hide the legacy AIMS login immediately after NEXUS authorization succeeds.
- Continue creating the existing AIMS designated-user session from the NEXUS identity.
