# NTTC Block 7 — Prescribed Form A + Physical Submission

## Approved changes
- `public/apps/nttc/` remains the authoritative NTTC module path.
- CoCIE Form A follows the uploaded prescribed two-page Form A structure instead of a redesigned government form.
- Applicant enters the complete name of the TVI Administrator/President endorser. The name is printed in the official `Endorsed by:` area; the handwritten signature/date remain for the printed form.
- Government-issued ID Type and No. is collected for the prescribed applicant declaration table.
- CAC focal schedules hard-copy submission after online approval.
- Applicant can confirm the schedule in the portal.
- CAC focal records arrival, verifies notarized Form A, hard-copy NC/TMC/IWER requirements, and original documents.
- Complete physical submission generates a claim stub number and printable claim stub.
- After physical receipt, status moves to Provincial Office processing.

## Block boundary
Storage remains the current local prototype abstraction. Production Google Sheets/Drive persistence and staff authorization will be connected in a backend integration block.
